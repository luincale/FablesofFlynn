The Netherese Age
− 3 3 9 D R & B E F O R E
The sky-empire of Netheril lifts its cities on Mythallars — orbs of raw magic that quietly fray the Weave.
One archmage's ambition will doom them all, and turn a border watchtower into a prison of planes.
before
del Thristen raises Larkspur
Carrolyn del Thristen, the youngest mage ever named to Netheril's High Council, wins fame by
building a Mythallar strong enough to lift the whole floating city of Larkspur — at the cost of only
a single life. At school he had befriended the exiled fey Orphan Prince of Dreams, Dante
Saturnid, and the two remain lifelong research colleagues. Meanwhile House Wingsong — a
mortal noble line — warns that the Mythallars invite ruin, and is exiled from Larkspur before
Karsus ever works his folly, raising Wingsong Manor as their seat on the empire's western edge.
FIGURES Carrolyn del Thristen · Dante Saturnid · House Wingsong

−339 DR
Karsus's Folly
An archmage's attempt to seize the power of the goddess of magic herself collapses the Weave.
Netheril falls from the sky in an instant, and the floating city of Larkspur crashes to earth — at
the very spot where the Larkspur Library will one day stand.
FALLOUT Netheril destroyed · Larkspur grounded
Per the Candlekeep Annals: the goddess of magic gave her life rather than let Karsus hold her
power, and was reborn as Mystra. Arcane magic itself briefly died with her — the true cause, the Annals
say, of Netheril's fall.
❧
−338 DR
Refuge at Wingsong Manor
del Thristen and Dante lead Larkspur's shattered survivors west to Wingsong Manor — seat of
the exiled House Wingsong — the one roof still standing that can shelter them. Dante's illusion
magic helps hide the refugees from Netherese enemies. What starts as a plea for shelter will not
stay one for long.
FIGURES del Thristen · Dante · the survivors of Larkspur · House Wingsong
−335 DR
The Feywild demiplane
Drawing on the Mythallar's intrinsic magic, del Thristen tears open a Feywild demiplane. Dante
and some of the survivors move inside — and are unknowingly trapped there for years, as the
plane's warped time slips out of step with the waking world.
FIGURES Dante Saturnid · trapped survivors
−328 DR
Shadowfell & the Macroscian
del Thristen opens a matching Shadowfell demiplane and tethers a Macroscian — a "Long
Shadow" — within it, feeding it Larkspur's survivors to make it grow. In the same siege he kills
the manor's mortal defender, Lord Val'Orea Wingsong — head of the mortal House Wingsong,
not the couatl she was once believed to be. Robed monks who come seeking their lord — the
early Knights of Wingsong, robed in the colors of the rainbow and couatls all, an order that
predates House Wingsong itself — are turned away and told he is dead.
FIGURES The Macroscian · Val'Orea Wingsong (mortal) · the early Knights of Wingsong

-- 2 of 11 --

−327 DR
The Infinite Planar Cycler
del Thristen completes his masterwork: a machine that blends the "dipolar" fuel of Feywild and
Shadowfell into a limitless pool of mana. To power it he needs two living poles — so he betrays
his oldest friend, forcing Dante into an eternal slumber as the machine's pole of light, balanced
against the Macroscian's dark.
FIGURES del Thristen · Dante (light pole) · Macroscian (dark pole)
−289 DR
Shar's Shadovar repelled
The goddess Shar sends the Shadovar to find the tower. Convinced they covet the Mythallar
rather than his machine, del Thristen fights them off and seals Wingsong's doors permanently —
locking himself inside with his creations.
FIGURES Shar · the Shadovar · del Thristen
Per the Candlekeep Annals: Shar sheltered these very Shadovar within a hidden Shadowfell
enclave, Thultanthar — the City of Shade — where they would not return openly to Faerûn for well over
a thousand years.
❧
−250 DR
The archmage unravels
del Thristen loses control of the tower's clashing illusion and planar energies. Wingsong begins
flickering through planes and time, and its maker spirals into madness. By −221 DR his journals
fall silent altogether.
FIGURES del Thristen · Wingsong Tower

-- 3 of 11 --

The Long Silence
− 2 2 1 → 1 3 8 5 D R
For centuries the tower drifts unseen while the world forgets it. Far away, the pieces of the coming story
quietly fall into place.
347 DR
Mad ramblings resume
After more than five centuries of silence, del Thristen's journals restart — but the entries are the
ravings of a mind long gone, hinting that the archmage has decayed into a Nothic, a wretched
thing bound to forbidden knowledge.
FIGURES del Thristen (now a Nothic)
951 DR
Tresendar Manor falls
Far from the tower, orc raids empty Tresendar Manor near Phandalin. Its cellars will sit
abandoned for centuries — until they become the hideout of the Redbrands the Fables must
one day clear.
PLACE Tresendar Manor · Phandalin
1372 DR
The Shadovar return
Far to the north, an old enemy stirs back into the light of day.
Per the Candlekeep Annals: the Shadovar of Thultanthar — the City of Shade, hidden away by Shar
since her assault on Wingsong Tower — return openly to Faerûn after more than a thousand years of
silence. Somewhere, an old goddess's patience begins to stir again.
❧

-- 4 of 11 --

1385 DR
The Spellplague
The Spellplague sweeps the Realms and stills the tower's magic. Dante blinks into a half-
controlled dream — years later he will be reached by a young girl named Pim. The same
catastrophe lets drider forces overrun the deep-gnome city of Tingstone, driving the survivors
into the hidden refuge of Hideaway Bay.
FIGURES Dante · Pim · the deep gnomes of Tingstone
Emporium Conservatory archives: the Spellplague follows the murder of the goddess Mystra. The
Conservatory's own troubling annotation — struck through twice, then left standing — marks this as the
very year Asmodeus is recorded to ascend to full godhood.
❧
1451 DR
Mount Hotenow erupts
Fire and ash reshape the north — and quietly set a later stage.
Per the Candlekeep Annals: the eruption devastates the Neverwinter region and empties the village
of Thundertree — leaving it exactly the kind of forsaken ruin a young dragon would one day claim, and
the deep gnomes would one day need cleared.
❧
1487 DR
The Second Sundering
The world's magic steadies again, on the very eve of the Emporium's rise.
Per the Candlekeep Annals: the Weave, broken since the Spellplague, is repaired. Magic across
Faerûn steadies again — just a handful of years before a wizard out of Neverwinter opens the doors of
a certain Emporium.
❧

Rise of the Emporium
~ 5 Y E A R S A G O
A wizard out of Neverwinter builds a house of wonders in Waterdeep — and makes a bargain with a fire
sorceress that will echo across the planes.
Left to right: Zoey Drake, Commander of the Serpents · Damien Wingsong, Knight Commander ·

~5 yrs
The Emporium is founded
Finnegan Flynn is still building his dream of the Emporium of Wonders when the Lord's Alliance
calls him south to face Hariqua Na'Haran's Circle of Flame. With his three companions —
Damien Wingsong, Zoey Drake, and Bastion Drake — he cuts through the cult and turns the tide
of the battle in the Trollclaws.
FIGURES Finnegan Flynn · Damien · Zoey · Bastion · Hariqua Na'Haran

-- 6 of 11 --

~5 yrs
The Wish is bargained
In the final duel amid fire and storm, Flynn halts instead of striking Hariqua down. Knowing she
carries the reality-binding power of an efreeti's wish, he trades her freedom for a single immense
wish — his companions folding their own into his. Publicly the cult is "broken," and Flynn is
rewarded the Waterdeep barracks that becomes the Emporium of Wonders. What he truly
wished for, he never plainly names — a secret that will not surface for years.
FIGURES Flynn · Hariqua Na'Haran · Lord Sildar Hallwinter
since
Hariqua's vengeance
Back in the City of Brass, the Sultan judges Hariqua weak for trading a wish for her own life. He
exiles her, strips away her efreeti mantle, and lets her suffer hunger and burns she has never
known — then restores her power with a command: "Conquer Flynn and make Waterdeep
suffer." She returns with a cauldron of everlasting flame to rebuild the Circle of Flame.
FIGURES Hariqua Na'Haran · the Efreeti Sultan · City of Brass
The Fables
1 4 9 1 D R
A new band is drawn into Flynn's orbit — and their hunt for a lost Mythallar peels back a secret older
than the empires, reaching all the way to the gods.
Prologues
Oni & Hunting Flames
Bastion recruits the rogue Ty'Ny De'Ath and the blue-skinned druid Kaladin Tempest. They seize
a pirate ship for the Emporium, break an Oni's grip on the magic-banning isle of Tyl Myerda, and
destroy a Cult of Flame cell and its salamander near Inndale — earning their place under the
Emporium's banner.
FIGURES Ty'Ny De'Ath · Kaladin Tempest · Bastion Drake

-- 7 of 11 --

Ch. 1–3
Phandalin & Wave Echo Cave
Formally hired, the Fables break the Redbrands in Phandalin, unmask Iarno "Glasstaff" Albrek
as the puppet of the drow Nezznar — the Black Spider — and rescue Sildar Hallwinter. Then
they wrest the lost Mines of Phandelver from the Black Spider himself in Wave Echo Cave.
FIGURES Iarno "Glasstaff" · Nezznar the Black Spider · Sildar Hallwinter
Ch. 4–5
Soulfire Forge → Hamun Kost
The Fables survive their first clash with Hariqua's reborn cult at the Soulfire Forge and the
tragedy of Thornhold Keep — Hariqua's assault fell first on the keep's dwarven garrison, and
she slaughtered them where they stood, meaning to claim the fortress for her own. The Fables
and the Emporium broke her siege, too late to save the dwarves but in time to deny her the
keep. Once Hariqua fled back to the Fire Plane, Flynn himself arrived and, in a rare display of
his own might, laid low the Circle of Flame's hidden caverns outright — claiming the Soulfire
Forge as his prize. Then the Fables run down the Red Wizard necromancer Hamun Kost — the
murderer of Moira'Val's master Peer'Na — across Triboar, seizing a book on Wingsong Tower
and the fireflies that might reveal it.
FIGURES Hariqua Na'Haran · Flynn · Hamun Kost · Moira'Val · Peer'Na
Per the Candlekeep Annals: those were the shield dwarves of Clan Stoneshaft, who had held
caverns beneath Thornhold — reached by a passage into the Underdark — since long before the keep
briefly fell to the Zhentarim in 1368 DR. That their generations-long stewardship should end at a fire
genasi's hand struck the Conservatory's scholars as a bitter, final page to a very old story — and left the
Emporium as Thornhold's first uncontested holder in all its long history.
❧
Ch. 6
Fireflies in the Underdark
Deep beneath the world the Fables find Hideaway Bay, the last deep gnomes of drowned
Tingstone. In exchange for guiding them to a new home at Thundertree, the elders share the
lore of the Deepglow fireflies — the key to locating the tower — and the engineer Gypsa joins
the crew, soon to captain their skyship.
FIGURES Gypsa · Chief Tackkl · the deep gnomes

-- 8 of 11 --

Thundertree
Peace at Thundertree
The gnomes' promised home is not yet safe: the ruined village of Thundertree is held by a young
green dragon — one of Hariqua's own cult allies. With the region already calmer after the
Fables' earlier rout of the Zhentarim in Phandalin, the druid Kaladin Tempest leads the retaking,
and the Fables slay the dragon. The victory gives the deep gnomes a peaceful place to settle
and severs one of the Circle of Flame's alliances.
FIGURES Kaladin Tempest · the green dragon · the deep gnomes
Emporium Conservatory archives, filed by Beratrymus Villium: Thundertree has stood empty
and wild since Mount Hotenow's eruption devastated the region a generation past — precisely the ruin
a young dragon would claim as its own. The Conservatory's notes even venture a name for the beast:
Venomfang.
❧
Ch. 7
Plague at Larkspur Heath
At Larkspur Heath the Fables find the great Library sealed and its scholars dying of a mystery
poison, held by Sir Bently Rhammer on the orders of Lord Neebo Wimbleblat. The standoff turns
to slaughter: Rhammer falls, the disgraced Newton Filz is exposed as a Zhentarim agent and
killed, and the poison is traced to Wimbleblat-marked supplies. The librarians are healed and
raised.
FIGURES Sir Bently Rhammer · Newton Filz · Lord Neebo Wimbleblat
Per the Candlekeep Annals: true resurrection has lately been no simple matter across Faerûn — a
death curse abroad in the world has been known to turn such magic hollow. That the Larkspur
librarians rose at all, the Conservatory notes, may say something quiet about Flynn's own hand in the
working.
❧
Ch. 8
Wingsong Tower found
The Fables pass through the tower's Fey and Shadow demiplanes and destroy the Long
Shadow to close the Shadowfell tear. Guided by the Raven Queen — speaking through Val'Orea
Wingsong's memory — they reach the Anchor Chamber, where the awakened Dante slays his
old friend del Thristen at last. The Fables escape with the Mythallar as the Zhentarim close in
and seize Damien and two knights.
FIGURES The Raven Queen · Dante · del Thristen · the Zhentarim

-- 9 of 11 --

Return
Thornhold & the reckoning
Breaking the Zhentarim's ring, the Fables free Damien and his couatl knights — an ancient order
that took the Wingsong name generations before Damien, who carries no blood tie to the family
himself — and sail the Fables' Fortune home to Thornhold. There Flynn reveals the truth: he is a
living dream of the primordial Jazirian, his twin is Asmodeus — also called Ahriman — and the
Mythallars long ago fractured the Pact Primordial. His Wish, he admits, sought access to
Ahriman's own power so he might outpace his brother and mend the Pact first — but it misfired,
hinting at a missing fulcrum between their twinned natures. He had prepared for years, studying
fiends and beholders whose powers echo Ahriman's — for as Jazirian's dream he is bound to
pure positive energy, and feared how the Negative Energy Plane might warp him. For now the
bond hides Flynn from Ahriman's sight, even as something foreboding seems to stand between
them. Bastion names five gathering storms: the Silver Marches mist, the Zhentarim's Peregost,
the active Well of Dragons, blood in Baldur's Gate, and Shar at the edge of sight.
FIGURES Flynn / Jazirian · Asmodeus (Ahriman) · Shar · the Peregost
Per the Candlekeep Annals: the Silver Marches have lately seen both orc-horde war and demon
lords loose from the Abyss — either could be the true root of Bastion's gray mist. The Well of Dragons,
meanwhile, is where the Cult of the Dragon has gathered to raise Tiamat herself from the Nine Hells —
if the smoke there is theirs, that storm is graver than he knows. The Annals also whisper of a town near
Baldur's Gate once dragged bodily into Avernus, Asmodeus's own dominion — a closer shadow of
Ahriman than Flynn has admitted.
❧
Emporium Conservatory archives: Thornhold has never known a quiet ownership — raised after
the Second Trollwar, held in turn by Tyrran knights, a noble Waterdhavian family, and briefly the
Zhentarim. But with the Stoneshaft dwarves who once held its underground halls slaughtered by
Hariqua's own hand, and no rival ever enforcing an older claim, the Emporium now holds Thornhold
as no one has before it — outright, and unopposed.
❧
what comes next
The road to Warlock's Crypt
How many Mythallars survive, and where, is unknown even to the Emporium. But Flynn and
Bastion have a lead: they send the Fables to parlay with Larloch, the Shadow King — the
ancient lich enthroned at Warlock's Crypt near the Sword Coast. Larloch is said to hold the
Moon Orb, and Flynn's company is confident the Orb is in truth a mythallar. The search
continues.
FIGURES Larloch the Shadow King · the Moon Orb · the Fables
Emporium Conservatory archives: Larloch is one of the very few Netherese archwizards who
survived Karsus's Folly under his own power, rather than by del Thristen's contrivances — perhaps the
only living witness to that whole age who is not a prisoner of Wingsong Tower.
❧
